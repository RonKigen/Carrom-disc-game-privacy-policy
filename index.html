Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Thank you for playing Carrom Disc Game ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by Carrom Disc Game.

We respect your privacy and strive to collect as little data as necessary to provide a smooth gaming experience:

Our app may integrate with third-party platforms (such as app store billing services for optional in-game purchases). These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data. Most game mechanics, physics calculations, and state management happen locally on your device or through secure encrypted channels.

Our game is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without appropriate parental consent.
